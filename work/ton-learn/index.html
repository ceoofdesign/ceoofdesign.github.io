TON Learn: learning Web3 inside Telegram

A self-initiated concept: a Telegram Mini App that teaches people the basics of TON before they lose money to a phishing link.

Type
Personal concept
Year
2025
My role
Idea, research, UX and UI
Platform
Telegram Mini App

Context

The idea came to me at the TON Society Balkans kick-off in Belgrade in January 2025. People new to TON kept asking the same questions, and many had already met phishing bots.

Problem

Knowledge about TON is spread across chats and docs, and newcomers learn about wallet security too late. Asking them to install another app adds one more barrier.

What I did

  • Format. Chose a Telegram Mini App so learning happens where people already are.
  • Content structure. Broke smart contracts, wallets and jettons into short lessons with quizzes.
  • Motivation. Designed rewards with TON Society badges for finished courses.
  • UI. Built mobile patterns that follow Telegram’s own look.

Outcome

A proof of concept and pitch presented to the community. It is not a shipped product.
