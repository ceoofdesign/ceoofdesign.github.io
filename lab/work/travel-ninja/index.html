Travel Ninja: hotel booking built from scratch

I designed Travel Ninja, the group’s new hotel booking service, end to end: search, hotel pages, rooms and checkout.

Company
Firebird Tours group
Period
2023–2026
My role
Product designer
Team
Business analysts, developers

Context

Rail Ninja customers already booked trains through the group. Travel Ninja added hotels, with inventory from Expedia, so travellers could plan the rest of the trip in the same place.

Problem

A hotel search has to show a lot at once: price, location, rating, room types and the rules of each rate. People compare many options and leave if paying feels risky.

What I did

  • Search. Results list with a map, filters by price, rating and property type, and clear total prices.
  • Hotel page. Gallery, amenities, location and reviews in one view, with the reserve button always in reach.
  • Rooms. Room cards that show beds, size, view and cancellation rules side by side.
  • Checkout. A one-page booking form with the order summary next to it and a clear breakdown of taxes and fees.
  • System. Built on the shared design system, so the product launched with the same components as Rail Ninja.

Outcome

The service launched as part of the group’s products. The live site has changed since then; these are my original designs.
